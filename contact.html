Before you come

  • Pre-order meals: everything is cooked fresh to order
  • Pack a warm layer for cool highland evenings
  • Day visitors pay a gate fee; ask what it covers

Getting here

  1. Eldoret townTake the Kaptagat road out of town
    start
  2. Kaptagat roadContinue towards Kaptagat and Chepkorio
    about 25 km
  3. Kaptagat Farm ResortBeside the road at Kaptarakwa

By air: fly into Eldoret International Airport, about 30 minutes from the resort by road. We can help arrange a pickup.

From Nairobi: follow the A104 through Nakuru to Eldoret, then the Kaptagat road as above.
