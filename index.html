The resort

Kaptagat Farm Resort is a garden property at Kaptarakwa, beside the Kaptagat road. The cottages are built in local bush stone on a ten-sided plan, with windows from the ground to the roof frame. Around them are hedged gardens, ponds and picnic terraces, and the forest begins at the edge of the grounds.

Food, drink and the garden

Restaurant

Meals are cooked from scratch to order and served outdoors, on the lawns or in the bandas. Allow time, or pre-order when you book.

The Mill Bar

A ten-sided bar at the centre of the garden, with a large screen and plenty of seating.

Garden and forest

Walks through the hedged gardens, birdwatching, and picnics in the shade of the acacias.

Dine and explore

Events and groups

A two-storey decagon conference room for up to 200 people, a seminar block, six bush-stone bandas for private dining, and open lawns for weddings and team building. The dormitories suit clubs and training camps.
