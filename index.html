Slow days on the farm

Kaptagat is cool, green and quiet: a place for long breakfasts, muddy boots and nights by the fire.

Farm to table

Milk, eggs, vegetables and honey from the farm, cooked the day they are picked.

Forest on the doorstep

Trails lead from the gate into the Kaptagat forest, home to colobus monkeys and over 200 bird species.

Cool highland air

Crisp mornings and fireplace evenings, the climate that draws Kenya's champion runners here to train.

Kenyan hospitality

A small team who know every guest by name, plan your days and pack your picnic.
